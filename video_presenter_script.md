## Quick Tips for TTS Engines
1. **Pronunciation Guide for TTS Engines**:
   - *APIs* -> Pronounce as letters: "A-P-Is"
   - *RAGs* -> Pronounce as a word: "Rags"
   - *MCPs* -> Pronounce as letters: "M-C-Ps"
   - *LLMs* -> Pronounce as letters: "L-L-Ms"
   - *Antigravity* -> Pronounce as "Anti-gravity"
2. **Music Selection**: Choose a low-volume, upbeat electronic ambient background track.
